- **Slide Visual**: Infographic showing SME cash crunch timeline, uncollected invoices, and the "credit gap".

  > Yet, they operate with only 2.6 months of cash runway. Why? Because over 250,000 CNY in client receivables are overdue, and upcoming quarterly CIT prepayment and payroll cycles create a dangerous liquidity dip on Day 45.  
  > Traditional accounting software records this after it's too late. FinPilot predicts it 45 days in advance and proactively acts."

## Slide 3: Two Products in One (2:00 - 3:15)
- **Slide Visual**: Architecture split: 1) Universal Embeddable SDK (`loader.js`) and 2) FinPilot SME Platform.
- **Presenter Script**:
  > "We built FinPilot to solve two real-world needs simultaneously:  
  > First, for existing SMEs that already use Kingdee, Yonyou, or custom ERPs, we created an **Embeddable SDK (`loader.js`)**. With just one line of code, any management portal can drop in our AI Copilot with zero CSS interference.  
  > Second, for modern SMEs, we provide the **FinPilot Cockpit** — a full-featured financial management platform with native multi-agent intelligence."

## Slide 4: Multi-Agent Architecture & Deterministic Grounding (3:15 - 4:45)
- **Slide Visual**: Diagram of Orchestrator routing to 5 Specialists + 20 Tools.
- **Presenter Script**:
  > "FinPilot is not a wrapper around a single chat prompt. It is a orchestrated multi-agent network with 5 domain specialists:  
  > 1. Cash Flow & Runway Specialist  
  > 2. WeBank Financing Matcher  
  > 3. Financial Ratio Analyst  
  > 4. Invoicing & AR Collection Specialist  
  > 5. Tax & Compliance Specialist

## Slide 6: 8-Layer Enterprise Security Pipeline (6:00 - 7:15)
- **Slide Visual**: Security pipeline flowchart: PII Masking, Prompt Injection Filter, 3 Permission Tiers.
- **Presenter Script**:
  > "In financial AI, security is paramount. FinPilot enforces an 8-layer defense pipeline:  
  > - Real-time prompt injection detection blocks jailbreaks and system overrides.  
  > - PII sanitization redacts card numbers and phones.  
  > - Our **3-Tier Permission Hierarchy** strictly forbids the agent from moving money or changing bank accounts (Tier 3), while requiring human supervisor approval for drafted invoices (Tier 2).  
  > Every execution is recorded in an immutable audit ledger."
